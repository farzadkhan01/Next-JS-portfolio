# 3. Brand Voice & Writing Style

The portfolio must communicate like a confident, capable professional.

## Tone

- Professional.
- Clear.
- Approachable.
- Confident but not arrogant.
- Technically informed.
- Human and conversational where appropriate.

## Writing Rules

1. Use simple, understandable English.
2. Keep headlines concise and memorable.
3. Avoid unnecessarily complicated technical language.
4. Focus on real capabilities and practical outcomes.
5. Avoid exaggerated claims.
6. Avoid generic AI-generated expressions.
7. Do not repeat the same information across sections.
8. Prefer meaningful statements over marketing buzzwords.
9. Use action-oriented CTAs.
10. Write for international visitors.

## Avoid Expressions Such As

- Coding is my superpower.
- Turning coffee into code.
- Pixel-perfect wizard.
- Passionate coding enthusiast.
- I live and breathe technology.
- I create digital magic.

Avoid empty claims such as "the best developer" or "world-class engineer."
